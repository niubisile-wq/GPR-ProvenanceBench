# Narrative Section Skeleton 2026-08-10

## One-sentence argument

In ground-penetrating radar recognition, current executable evidence shows that environment and provenance structure can strongly reshape apparent generalization, with Res-SAM environment transfer providing the strongest cross-model support and Mojahid/4TU providing bounded secondary and stress-test evidence.

## Introduction contract

| Paragraph | Job | Target words | Topic contract | Must not claim |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| I1 | field scale | 160 | Open with why reliable ground-penetrating radar recognition matters beyond one curated image set. | Do not claim the field has solved automated GPR recognition or that this work is first. |
| I2 | bottleneck | 180 | Define the evaluation bottleneck: random or weakly structured splits can mix acquisition, environment and processing provenance. | Do not state that every published GPR model is leaked or invalid. |
| I3 | prior gap | 190 | Position the unresolved gap as a lack of executable, multi-asset, model-family-level provenance stress testing. | Do not imply the blind external gate is already closed. |
| I4 | present study | 170 | State what GPR-ProvenanceBench currently does and where its evidence stops. | Do not call the package submission-ready or externally validated. |

## Discussion contract

## Narrative risk map

| Risk | Trigger terms to avoid | Required replacement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overstating external validation | validated externally; blind test passed; independent external result | protocol-ready but not complete; open blind external gate |
| Overstating Mojahid | universal leakage; decisive split inflation | directionally consistent but modest/model-dependent split sensitivity |
| Overstating 4TU | causal proof; main confirmation; robust 4TU generalization | raw-trace-derived stress-test and failure-mode evidence |
| Overstating release readiness | public repository available; data deposited; source data complete | source-data and sanitized release skeletons exist, but DOI, licence and rights are unresolved |
| Overstating method replication | full Res-SAM replication; TIGPR core asset | Res-SAM lightweight asset is executable; full SAM checkpoint/runtime and TIGPR local executable asset remain NO-GO |

## Drafting boundary

This package is a paragraph-contract layer. It is safe to use for writing the Introduction, Discussion and Conclusion, but it is not final manuscript prose. Citations, rendered figures, repository identifiers, Reporting Summary answers and a real blind external result remain outside this package.
